# Loyalty Program Rules (v3, effective 2025-01-01)

## Membership tiers
- **Bronze**: default tier on enrolment.
- **Silver**: 1,500+ points earned in the trailing 12 months.
- **Gold**: 5,000+ points earned in the trailing 12 months. Gold members receive a 5% bonus on all points.

## Active member definition
A member is **active** in a calendar month if they have at least one transaction in that month.
A member is **lapsed** if they have had no transaction for 90 consecutive days, and **churned**
after 180 days without a transaction.

## Points
- 1 point per $1 of eligible spend (excluding lottery, tobacco, gift cards).
- Campaign multipliers stack additively with the Gold bonus, never multiplicatively.
- Points expire 24 months after they are earned.

## Privacy
Loyalty card numbers and e-mail addresses are personal information and must never be
displayed in analytical outputs. Refer to members by `customer_id`.
