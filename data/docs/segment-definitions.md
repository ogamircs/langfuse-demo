# Customer Segment Definitions

Segments are re-scored quarterly from the trailing 12 months of baskets.

| Segment | Definition |
|---|---|
| Value Seeker | > 40% of spend on promoted or private-label items |
| Family Stock-Up | Average basket > $120 with ≥ 15 lines |
| Fresh Foodie | > 45% of spend in Produce, Meat, Bakery, Dairy |
| Convenience | ≥ 8 visits per month, average basket < $35 |
| Occasional | Fewer than 2 visits per month |

## Lapse-risk heuristic
A member is **at risk of lapsing** when their most recent transaction is between 45 and 89 days
old (they are not yet lapsed at 90 days). Prioritise Gold and Silver members for win-back offers.
