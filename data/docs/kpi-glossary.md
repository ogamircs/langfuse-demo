# KPI Glossary

- **Revenue**: sum of `line_amount` across transaction lines (net of discounts, before tax).
- **Basket size**: revenue per distinct `txn_id`.
- **Private-label share**: revenue from products with `is_private_label = true` divided by total revenue.
- **Revenue per square foot**: store revenue in the period divided by `stores.sqft`.
- **Points liability**: outstanding points × $0.001 redemption value.
- **Campaign day**: each calendar day between `start_date` and `end_date` inclusive.
